## What this is (and is not)

This is **not** a generic chatbot or a $20/mo widget.

It is a **managed AI phone intake layer** for **retail vape shops** — answers inbound calls, handles common questions, captures leads and wholesale inquiries, and sends you a **structured summary** after every call — without hiring extra front-desk staff or missing after-hours customers.

**What it does today (demo):**
- Answers inbound calls immediately, 24/7 (or after-hours only — your choice at setup)
- Discloses that it is an AI assistant (never pretends to be human)
- Handles common caller types: customer inquiry, hours/location, product question, wholesale/vendor, complaint, other
- Shares **configured** store info: hours, address, directions, general product categories
- Captures contact details for follow-up (name, phone, email, what they need)
- Flags wholesale / bulk / vendor inquiries for owner review
- **Does not** sell to minors, confirm age without your policy workflow, guarantee specific stock without integration, or quote prices you're not configured to share
- Produces a **structured summary** after every call (email alert to your team)

**What we add after kickoff (production):**
- Custom script with **your** brands, devices, disposables, e-liquid lines, and house rules
- SMS or email alerts to owner/manager on every call (or only high-value ones)
- Weekly call summary: volume, top questions, missed opportunities
- Optional: hours sync, holiday closures, promo mentions
- Monthly script tuning from real call patterns
