## What this is (and is not)

This is **not** a generic AI phone bot or a $199/mo SaaS widget.

It is a **managed project intake and coordination layer** built for owners who run **commercial renovation and tenant improvement** through outside design, construction, and broker partners — without hiring a full-time coordinator or forcing everyone onto enterprise construction software.

**What it does today (demo):**
- Answers inbound calls immediately, 24/7
- Discloses that it is an AI assistant (never pretends to be human)
- Identifies caller type: new lead, vendor/subcontractor, broker/tenant, existing contact
- Qualifies new project inquiries: type, location, scope, budget, timeline, decision-maker
- Captures vendor updates: project, status, milestone, blockers, approval needed
- Produces a **structured summary** after every call (email alert to your team)
- Stays in bounds: **does not quote pricing, approve work, issue change orders, or give legal/engineering/permit advice**
- Escalates safety issues and instructs callers in immediate danger to call 911

**What we add after kickoff (production):**
- Routing to partner buckets (GC, designer, broker, property manager, owner approval)
- Owner alerts when **your decision** is required (with schedule impact noted)
- Vendor follow-up in a consistent format ("milestone, date, blocker")
- Project board / intake log — every call and update in one place
- Weekly executive summary: decisions due, open blockers, completed intake
- Monthly script and routing tuning based on real calls
